# Modules

## ERP
Customer PO, PO lines, products, BOM, customers, suppliers.

## PMC
Work order, work order coding, route assignment, schedule, delivery risk.

## WMS
Receiving, IQC status, storage, smart shelf, picking, line issue, return, scrap.

## MES
SMT execution, feeder binding, material anti-error, first article, output, WIP.

## Equipment Management
Equipment master data, equipment status, preventive maintenance, breakdown log, calibration, spare parts, and downtime control. This sits under MES and shares traceability with quality and audit.

## Quality
IQC, SPI, AOI, ICT, repair, rework, defects, abnormal closure.

## Traceability
PO, PO line, work order, PCB SN, material reel, station, machine, operator, inspection, shipment.

## Reports
Output, yield, OEE, inventory, material usage, WIP, delivery, quality loss.

## Finance / Accounting
Chart of accounts, cost centers, profit centers, fiscal periods, currency, exchange rates, VAT, AP/AR references, shipments, inventory valuation, work order cost accumulation, journal entries.

## Admin
Users, roles, permissions, settings, audit logs.
